FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

A few clear answers so users understand what NiiveshNeeti Recommendation is and what it is not.

Is this a tips channel?
No. NiiveshNeeti provides SEBI compliant research Recommendation. Calls are backed by analysis, structure and risk logic—this is not random tips or guaranteed profit service.
Which instruments do you primarily cover?
We focus on index options and related levels—primarily Nifty, Bank Nifty, Finnifty and relevant index structures, with occasional index-based futures context.
Who should join NiiveshNeeti?
Active index option traders, expiry traders, and serious learners who want structured calls, levels, risk-defined trades and real-time support instead of noise.
Will I get daily trade setups?
Yes. Depending on the plan, you get 1–3 research-based index option trade setups daily along with levels, bias and brief notes on context.
Do you cover hedging and risk management?
Yes. We actively highlight risk, stoploss, position sizing, and when to reduce exposure or switch to defined-risk strategies in high volatility phases.
Do you execute trades on my behalf?
No. This is only research and Recommendation. You remain fully responsible for execution, risk and your capital. We do not provide PMS, execution or guaranteed returns.

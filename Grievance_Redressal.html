1. Resolution Timeline

The above details are intended to help you approach the concerned Research Analyst first. We aim to resolve all grievances within 21 working days from the date of receipt.

2. Escalation to SCORES

If your grievance is not resolved within the 21-day timeframe, or if you are unsatisfied with the resolution, you can escalate it to SEBI’s SCORES Platform (SEBI Complaint Redressal System).

Visit SCORES Portal
